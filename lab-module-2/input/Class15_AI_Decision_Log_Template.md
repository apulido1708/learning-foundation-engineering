## Final checks
- [ ] Every accepted change is supported by approved requirements or an explicitly approved new decision.
- [ ] Unconfirmed business rules are recorded as open questions, not approved requirements.
- [ ] Interface contracts, sequence diagrams and data models are consistent where they overlap.
- [ ] Relevant preconditions, postconditions, failure behavior and invariants are considered.
- [ ] No confidential or personal information was sent to an unauthorized external AI service.
- [ ] The team can explain and defend every accepted design decision.
